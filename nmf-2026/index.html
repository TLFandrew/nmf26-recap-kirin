At the bar

Kirin Ichiban at both NightMarket.fun bars.

Kirin Ichiban was the only beer served at NightMarket.fun 2026. It poured at $8 at both the general bar and the VIP bar, and Kirin decor dressed both spaces.

Built for a night out.

The bar was 21 and over, and we made it easy for guests to leave the car at home, with free CapMetro Red Line passes, a free shuttle from Lakeline Station, and Cedar Park's LiNK rides. Rain arrived around 8:30 pm and we closed early around 9:30, cutting the bar's last hour short.

2027

Kanpai to what's next.

2026 was Kirin's third year at NightMarket.fun, and our first working directly with the Kirin team. This fall we proposed a Kirin kanpai activation in the Passport app: guests scan a Kirin QR code at the bar, a kanpai animation from Kirin Ichiban plays, and they collect a Kirin mahjong tile they can redeem for Kirin swag. We didn't get it built in time for this year's festival, and we'd love to bring it to life in 2027 with an earlier start.

Because the bar is 21 and over, every scan would be a verified of-age guest engaging with Kirin, and we'd report the scans back to you after the event.

We'd love to talk about growing with Kirin across NightMarket.fun and the Austin Dragon Boat Festival in 2027.
